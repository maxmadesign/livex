Sphere Ripples

Rays leave the centre of an invisible sphere. Where each one hits the surface, a ring spreads out, thins and fades, then starts again somewhere else.

Effect

150 ripples are on the sphere at once, spread evenly over its surface. Each is a 0.5 s ray, then a 1–2 s ring; when it fades it is reborn in a new direction 0–0.5 s later.

Ripples fill the sphere from the bottom up, and the number in the middle is the progress. A slice of a sphere has an area proportional only to its height, so when the bright line is 40% of the way up, exactly 40% of the surface is covered. The faint rings mark every 10%.
